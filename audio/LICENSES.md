Everything in `audio/` is generated by the scripts in `tools/audio/` (see `docs/audio-direction.md`).

|---|---|---|
| `music/*.mp3`, `sfx/jingle_*.mp3` | Original score written in code (`tools/audio/score.py`), rendered offline with FluidSynth using the **MS Basic** General MIDI SoundFont that ships with MuseScore 4 (`MS Basic.sf3`) | Score: same license as this repository. SoundFont: MIT (MuseScore_General by S. Christian Collins, based on FluidR3 by Frank Wen and FluidR3Mono by Michael Cowgill); full notice in `MS_Basic_License.md` |
