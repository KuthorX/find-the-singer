# Audio sources and licenses

Everything in `audio/` was composed programmatically by AI (Claude) and generated by the scripts in `tools/audio/` (see `docs/audio-direction.md`). The music and SFX are rendered offline with Vital, Serum 2 and the MS Basic SoundFont. Nothing was recorded, and no sample packs or Kontakt/NI libraries are used.

| Files | Source | License |
|---|---|---|
| `music/*.ogg`, `sfx/jingle_*.ogg` | Original score written in code (`tools/audio/score.py`, orchestrated in `tools/audio/orchestra.py`). Rendered offline with: **Serum 2** factory presets (VOX - Synth Pop Choir, PL - Xylo Pluck, WIND - Flute, KY - Delicacy, KY - Harpsichord, BL - Kinderjoy); **Vital** with the "In The Mix" preset pack (Strings Section); and **FluidSynth + MS Basic** General MIDI SoundFont (finger and acoustic bass, drum kit). Effects are pedalboard built-ins (reverb, delay, filters) | Score: same license as this repository. Vital: GPL-3.0 synthesizer (Matt Tytel); rendered audio is our own production.
